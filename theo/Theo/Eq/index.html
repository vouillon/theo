Module Theo.Eq

The theory of equality (=, <>).

Atoms are equalities v = c; v <> c is their negation.

Semantics: Theo knows only that a variable equals at most one constant, so it reasons as if C.t had infinitely many values: any conjunction of disequalities is satisfiable. The answers are exact when C.t is infinite, such as string or int. For a finite type, they are sound but may be incomplete, as for Leq: unsatisfiability, tautologies and implications that Theo reports hold, but over {A, B}:

  • v = A || v = B is not a tautology;
  • v <> A && v <> B is satisfiable, and sat returns it as a model.

For a type with few values, a boolean variable per value, constrained to exactly one being true, gives exact answers.
